macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple Silicon and Intel.

This is a test build, not a release. It is signed by Technoverge, but it has not been notarized by Apple yet, so macOS will refuse to open it and show a warning instead. That is Gatekeeper doing its job: notarization is Apple confirming it scanned the app, and without a ticket nobody has checked this one.

If you want to try it anyway, the steps are below. If you would rather wait for a notarized build, that is the sensible choice.

Download Headvane 0.1.1 · 1.2 MB

Installing a test build

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Headvane to Applications.
  2. Open it once. macOS will block it.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Headvane.

On macOS 15 and later, right-clicking and choosing Open no longer works for this. The Privacy & Security panel is the only route.

What it will ask for

PermissionWhy
CameraTo measure the angle of your head. Nothing is recorded, and the image never leaves your Mac.
AccessibilityTo bring a window forward. This is the only supported way on macOS for any app to focus another app's window.

The Accessibility permission is granted to an app's signing identity, so it now persists across updates. Earlier builds were signed ad-hoc, which produced a new identity every time and made macOS ask again after each update.

Uninstalling

Quit Headvane from its menu and drag it to the Trash. To remove its settings and calibration too, delete the Headvane folder in ~/Library/Application Support, and remove its entries under Camera and Accessibility in System Settings.
