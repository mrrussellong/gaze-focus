Keyboard focus follows the screen you face

A menu bar app for macOS with more than one display.

Turn to another monitor and type. Headvane moves keyboard focus to that screen's window, so what you type lands where you are looking instead of where you left off.

It reads the angle of your head through the camera on your Mac. It does not record anything, it has no account, and it does not know what is on your screens.

What it does not do

Worth stating plainly, because the opposite is usually assumed.

  • It does not track your eyes. It works out which display you are facing. It cannot tell where on that display you are looking.
  • It does not read your keystrokes. It asks macOS how long it has been since you last typed, so it can wait for you to stop. It never sees which keys.
  • It does not read your window titles or your screen contents.
  • It does not record or transmit video. Each camera frame becomes two numbers — a horizontal angle and a vertical one — and is then discarded.

What it needs

PermissionWhy
CameraTo measure the angle of your head. Nothing is recorded.
AccessibilityTo raise and focus a window belonging to another app. This is the only supported way for any app to do that on macOS.

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Two or more displays, or it has nothing to switch between.
