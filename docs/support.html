Before you write

Focus is not moving at all

  1. Check the menu bar item. It says why it is not switching — waiting for you to stop typing, cannot see you, paused, and so on. That line is usually the answer.
  2. Check that Accessibility is granted in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. Without it, no app can focus another app's windows. If you have just updated Headvane, macOS sometimes needs the switch turned off and on again.
  3. Check that the screen you are turning to has been set up. A monitor you added after setting up is not yet calibrated.

It switches when I did not mean it to

Raise How long a look must last and How far you must turn in Settings. Both make the app more reluctant. Most people who find it twitchy are sitting closer to their monitors than the defaults assume.

It switches to the wrong screen

Set your screens up again. Calibration encodes where you sat and where the monitors were; nudging a monitor or raising your chair invalidates it. If two monitors are very close together, or at nearly the same angle from where you sit, there may not be enough difference between them to tell apart reliably — that is a limit of the approach, not a setting.

One app's windows never take focus

Some applications decline to let another app focus their windows. Tell us which app and which window, and it may be fixable for that app specifically.

What to include

  • Your macOS version, and your Mac model.
  • How many displays, roughly where they sit, and which are built in.
  • What the menu bar item says when the problem is happening.
  • What you expected instead.

Uninstalling

Quit Headvane from its menu, then drag it to the Trash. To remove its settings and calibration as well, delete the Headvane folder in ~/Library/Application Support. Revoking Camera and Accessibility access in System Settings is worth doing too — macOS keeps those entries after an app is deleted.
