Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Headvane runs entirely on your Mac. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server that holds anything about you. We do not have a copy of your calibration, your settings, your screen layout or your camera.

The camera

Headvane opens the camera to measure which way your head is turned.

  • Frames are processed on your Mac, by macOS's own on-device vision framework.
  • Each frame is reduced to two numbers — a horizontal angle and a vertical angle — and the frame is then released. Nothing derived from the image beyond those two numbers is kept, written to disk or sent anywhere.
  • There is no recording feature, no preview that is saved, and no code path that could upload a frame.
  • The camera is switched off, not merely ignored, whenever your screen is locked, your Mac sleeps, or you pause the app.

Typing and the mouse

Headvane waits for a pause in your typing before it moves focus, which means it has to know whether you are typing. It asks macOS how long ago the last keyboard event happened. It does not install a keyboard or mouse listener, so it cannot see which keys you pressed, and it does not request the Input Monitoring permission that such a listener would need.

The app's pause shortcut is registered with the system as a single specific key combination. The system delivers only that combination to the app; no other keystroke reaches it.

Your windows

To move focus, Headvane needs to know the position and size of windows and which app owns them. It reads that. It does not read window titles, which is why it never asks for the Screen Recording permission.

No telemetry. No crash reporting. No advertising or marketing identifiers. No device fingerprint beyond the installation identifier described above, which is a random value created on your Mac and means nothing outside your licence.

If you never enter a licence key and turn update checks off, Headvane makes no network requests at all.

Diagnostics

Headvane writes a short activity log on your Mac so that problems can be diagnosed. It stays on your Mac. If you choose to send it to us when asking for help, that is an ordinary email from you, and you can read the file first.

Children

Headvane is a productivity tool for people who buy software. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

We hold almost nothing. What exists is: your purchase record and licence key, held by our payment provider, and any email you have sent us. To ask what that is, to correct it, or to have it deleted, write to support@headvane.com. Deleting your purchase record also deletes the licence it belongs to, so your licence will stop working.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what the app sends, the change will be described in the release notes of the version that makes it, not only here.
